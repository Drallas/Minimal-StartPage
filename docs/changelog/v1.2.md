# v1.2

Since v1.1: AI shortcuts become a checklist of up to five services, search and AI links can open in a new tab, the phone layout keeps the search in the middle, and there are clearer limits and icons throughout.

## Highlights

- **AI shortcuts**: a checklist under Data beheren: Claude, ChatGPT, Grok, Duck.ai, Lumo, Kagi and Meta AI. Five at most, your own shortcuts included. Each shows its icon, in the same monochrome style.
- **New tab**: under Advanced, search can open in a new tab (off by default), and AI links in a new tab (on by default).
- **Limits**: links, time zones, AI shortcuts and weather places show how many are in use, such as 4/5, and adding is greyed out when full.
- **Resetting**: the reset buttons sit in Advanced, and reset settings asks for confirmation first.
- **Phones**: the search stays in the middle; the clock, the AI row and the quote go around it.
- **Minimal**: keeps the colour button at the bottom left.
