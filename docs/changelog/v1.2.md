# v1.2

Since v1.1: AI shortcuts become a checklist of up to five services, search and AI links can open in a new tab, the phone layout keeps the search in the middle, and there are clearer limits, warnings and icons throughout.

## Highlights

- **AI shortcuts**: tick the services you want under Data beheren: Claude, ChatGPT, Grok, Duck.ai, Lumo, Kagi and Meta AI. Five at most, your own shortcuts included. Each shows its icon, in the same monochrome style.
- **New tab**: under Advanced, open search in a new tab (off by default), and AI links in a new tab (on by default).
- **Limits**: links, time zones, AI shortcuts and weather places each show how many are in use, such as 4/5, and grey out adding when full.
- **Resetting**: the reset buttons sit next to "Back to basic settings" in Advanced, and reset settings asks for confirmation first.
- **Phones**: the search stays in the middle; the clock, the AI row and the quote go around it.
- **Minimal**: keeps the colour button at the bottom left.
- **Help**: export and import have their own section, and the AI shortcuts are explained.

## Privacy

Weather sends your IP address to ipapi.co, or the city you choose to Open-Meteo, only while weather is on. Showing your IP address uses the same lookup. Extra places send their name to Open-Meteo. A custom AI shortcut loads its icon from its own website (/favicon.ico) when it is added, so that site sees your visit. Nothing else leaves the page. Search and AI links go to the service you choose when you click.

## Notes

Tested in Chromium and with simulated phone widths. Not yet checked on a real iPhone or Android device, or in Safari and Brave. The German, French, Spanish and Chinese texts still need a native-speaker check.
