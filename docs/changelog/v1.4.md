# v1.4

The latest release. Since v1.3: the limits show only when they are reached, export and import have their own tab, the reset options are grouped and marked as dangerous, and the settings show the version.

## Changes after the v1.4 tag (newest first)

- **1.4.6**: a Quick start window explains the search engine icon (with a red arrow to it), AI links and the three pages. It opens from the first-visit card, the basic settings and Help.
- **1.4.5**: the version line in the settings links to the changelog.
- **1.4.4**: a one-time hint on a first visit points to the gear and the ? key. It goes for good after one click, the ? key or opening the settings.
- **1.4.3**: the red frame moves onto the two reset buttons themselves, so they are the same height as the others.
- **1.4.2**: the reset buttons are normal height, and Manage data sits beside them without a red frame.
- **1.4.1**: the count under the weather places is gone; the full state shows in the add field in Manage data.

## Highlights

- **Limits**: the links, time zones, AI shortcuts and weather places show nothing about their limit until it is reached. Then the add field says so in its own middle, for example "5 of 5 · full", and the add button is greyed out. The maximum is stated only once.
- **Button states**: buttons that can be used are a darker grey than those that cannot. Arrows, pencils and crosses keep their look. Red is used only for the reset buttons.
- **Counters**: green while there is room, blue with "full" at the limit, both in soft tones.
- **Backup tab**: export and import have their own tab, the last one in Manage data, with a short explanation. Export saves your settings and data to a file; import either merges the file with what you have or replaces everything.
- **Reset**: the reset options sit at the bottom of Advanced, under one red heading. Reset settings brings back the defaults and keeps your data. Delete my data removes your own links, AI shortcuts, time zones, city and extra places, and asks for confirmation.
- **Version**: the bottom of the basic settings shows the version and the date of the last change.
- **Advanced on phones**: no more horizontal scrolling. Short option groups sit two to a row, and the date labels are shorter.
- **Hint**: on a first visit, a small card points to the settings. (Added in 1.4.4.)

## Fixes

- The Background heading in Advanced had no text in any language.
- The limit messages no longer repeat the maximum in the labels and in the messages below.
