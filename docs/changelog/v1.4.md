# v1.4

Since v1.3: the lists in Manage data show their limit only when it is reached, export and import have their own tab, the reset options are grouped and marked as dangerous, and the settings show the version. The phone layout of Advanced is tidier, and the documentation has screenshots.

## Highlights

- **Manage data**: the links, time zones, AI shortcuts and weather places show nothing about their limit until it is reached. Then the add field says so in its own middle, for example "5 of 5 · full", and the add button is greyed out. Buttons that can be used are a darker grey than those that cannot, and the maximum is stated only once.
- **Backup**: export and import have their own tab, the last one in Manage data. The tab explains what each does and holds both buttons. Export saves your settings and data to a file on your computer; import either merges that file with what you have or replaces everything.
- **Reset**: the reset options sit at the bottom of Advanced, under one red heading. Reset settings brings back the defaults and keeps your data. Delete my data removes your own links, AI shortcuts, time zones, city and extra places, and cannot be undone, so the text asks you to make a backup on the Backup tab first.
- **Version**: the bottom of the basic settings shows the version and the date of the last change.
- **Advanced on phones**: no more horizontal scrolling. Short option groups sit two to a row, and the date labels are shorter.
- **Backgrounds**: the page's background pictures are described in the README under Look and feel, with a larger overview on the screenshots page.
- **Documentation**: the README has a Look and feel section and a link to the screenshots page, which shows the settings, Manage data and the Backup tab.
- **Release workflow**: publishing a release sets the version and the date in the app, and retakes the screenshots, through `.github/workflows/set-version.yml`.

## Fixes

- The Background heading in Advanced had no text in any language. It has a label now.
- The limit messages no longer repeat the maximum in the labels and in the messages below.

## Privacy

Nothing changed. Weather sends your IP address to ipapi.co, or the city you choose to Open-Meteo, only while weather is on. Showing your IP address uses the same lookup. Extra places send their name to Open-Meteo. A custom AI shortcut loads its icon from its own website (/favicon.ico) when it is added, so that site sees your visit. Nothing else leaves the page. Search and AI links go to the service you choose when you click.

## Notes

Tested in Chromium and with simulated phone widths. Not yet checked on a real iPhone or Android device, or in Safari and Brave. On a phone the Advanced panel still scrolls vertically, because it holds many options. The German, French, Spanish and Chinese texts still need a native-speaker check.
