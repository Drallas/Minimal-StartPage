# Before 1.0

Built between 9 and 10 October 2026, before the first release. Tags v1.0.0 and v1.0.1 were made on the way; v1.0.1 added an HTML check and a tag-triggered release workflow, which was later removed.

## Look and layout

- The clock sits above the search, with a short caption above the AI row. The footer and quote were tuned in size and contrast.
- A quote of the day, rotating every six hours, in the browser's language. Click it to see the next one. The pool was trimmed to 50 checked quotes.
- The wallpaper mode: photos hosted locally in three sizes, a tint slider, and wallpaper as the default.
- Landscape phones and phones with a browser bar are handled.

## Search and links

- Search engines: Kagi as the first default, DuckDuckGo later; the engine switcher shows logos.
- Permanent links with logos (Wikipedia, Google, Apple and others), your own links, and the Wikipedia button in the browser's language.
- AI shortcuts: Claude, ChatGPT, Grok and Duck.ai, with an editor; later a checklist.

## Time, date and weather

- The clock switches between 12 and 24 hours, and later a full time mode with seconds.
- The date switches between long and short, and shows the week and day of the year on hover.
- Optional weather, first from the IP address, later as one choice: IP address or a city. Privacy-first defaults for new visitors.
- Time zones behind a globe, up to five, sorted by UTC offset.

## Settings and data

- A settings panel, then a basic view and an Advanced view, with presets Minimal, Standard and Personal.
- A Data panel with tabs for links, time zones, AI shortcuts and weather places.
- Export and import of settings and data as one JSON file.
- A Help guide, first in settings, later with a gear icon in the top bar.

## Still open at this point

- Phones, Safari and Brave were tested only in Chromium and with simulated phone widths.
- The German, French, Spanish and Chinese texts were not yet checked by a native speaker.
