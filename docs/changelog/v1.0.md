# v1.0

The first release: a minimal start page with search, a clock and, if you want them, weather, time zones, links and AI shortcuts. Everything beyond the search is optional.

## Highlights

- **Pages**: Minimal and Standard presets. Personal shows when your own mix matches neither, and keeps that mix so you can switch back to it.
- **Settings**: a basic view with the pages and a Help link. Advanced holds every option in four groups.
- **Data panel**: links, time zones, AI shortcuts and weather places in one panel with four tabs.
- **Links**: a Wikipedia button in your browser's language, or the full links window with the logos and your own links.
- **AI shortcuts**: Claude, ChatGPT, Grok and Duck.ai by default. Change, reorder or remove them, up to five.
- **Weather**: optional, from your IP address or a city you choose. Up to three extra places, in time-zone order.
- **Time zones**: up to five, sorted by UTC offset, with a day difference.
- **Background**: wallpaper on or off, a tint slider, and a button to hide the wallpaper switch.
- **Export and import**: save your choices and your own data to one file, and load it on another device.
- **Six languages**: English, Dutch, German, French, Spanish and Chinese.
