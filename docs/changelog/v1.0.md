# v1.0

A minimal start page with search, a clock and, if you want them, weather, time zones, links and AI shortcuts. Everything beyond the search is optional, and nothing leaves the page unless you turn on weather or your IP address.

## Highlights

- **Pages**: Minimal and Standard presets. Personal shows when your own mix matches neither, and keeps that mix so you can switch back to it.
- **Settings**: a basic view with the pages, a privacy note and a Help link. Advanced holds every option in four groups.
- **Data panel**: links, time zones, AI shortcuts and weather places in one panel with four tabs. Each opens on its own tab.
- **Links**: a Wikipedia button in your browser's language, or the full links window with the logos and your own links.
- **AI shortcuts**: Claude, ChatGPT, Grok and Duck.ai by default. Change, reorder or remove them, up to five.
- **Weather**: optional, from your IP address or a city you choose. Up to three extra places, shown in time-zone order.
- **Time zones**: up to five, sorted by UTC offset, with a day difference.
- **Background**: wallpaper on or off, a tint slider, and a button to hide the wallpaper switch.
- **Export and import**: save your choices and your own data to one JSON file, and load it on another device.
- **Six languages**: English, Dutch, German, French, Spanish and Chinese. The German, French, Spanish and Chinese texts still need a native-speaker check.

## Privacy

Weather sends your IP address to ipapi.co, or the city you choose to Open-Meteo; extra places send their name to Open-Meteo. The IP address line sends your address to ipapi.co. Both are off by default and are described in the Help and the README.

## Notes

Tested in Chromium and with simulated phone widths. Not yet checked on a real iPhone or Android device, or in Safari.
