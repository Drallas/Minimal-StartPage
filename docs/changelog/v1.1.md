# v1.1

Since v1.0: a Standard page as the default, export and import that merge instead of overwriting, a Links window, a language choice in Advanced settings, and a Help window with an About section and a link to the FAQ.

## Highlights

- **Import merges**: links and AI shortcuts are matched by address, time zones and weather places by name. Duplicates are skipped; choose Overwrite to replace everything instead.
- **Pages**: Minimal has nothing extra, Standard is the default, and Personal keeps your own mix.
- **Background**: wallpapers with photo credits, and a tint slider that starts at neutral.
- **Weather**: up to five extra places, with city suggestions while you type.
- **Time zones**: their own window, sorted by UTC offset.
- **Links**: permanent links with logos, grouped, plus your own links.
- **Export**: always downloads as a file.
- **Language**: choose the interface language under Advanced settings.

## Privacy

Weather sends your IP address to ipapi.co, or the city you choose to Open-Meteo; extra places send their name to Open-Meteo. The IP address line sends your address to ipapi.co. Both are off by default and are described in the Help and the README.

## Notes

Tested in Chromium and with simulated phone widths. Not yet checked on a real iPhone or Android device, or in Safari.
