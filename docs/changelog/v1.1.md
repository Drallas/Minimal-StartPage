# v1.1

Since v1.0: a Standard page as the default, export and import that merge instead of overwriting, a Links window, and a language choice in Advanced settings.

## Highlights

- **Import merges**: links and AI shortcuts are matched by address, time zones and weather places by name. Duplicates are skipped. Overwrite replaces everything instead.
- **Pages**: Minimal has nothing extra, Standard is the default, and Personal keeps your own mix.
- **Background**: wallpapers with photo credits, and a tint slider that starts at neutral.
- **Weather**: up to five extra places, with city suggestions while you type.
- **Time zones**: their own window, sorted by UTC offset.
- **Links**: permanent links with logos, grouped, plus your own links.
- **Export**: always downloads as a file.
- **Language**: the interface language is chosen under Advanced settings.
- **Help**: an About section and a link to the questions and answers.
