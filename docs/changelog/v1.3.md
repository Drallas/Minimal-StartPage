# v1.3

Since v1.2: the clock and the date can be set in Advanced, the date follows your browser, Minimal is leaner, and the icons, the phone layout and the documentation are tidier.

## Highlights

- **Clock**: choose 24 hours, 12 hours (AM/PM) or full time with seconds in Advanced, or click the time as before. The colons stay still.
- **Date**: choose the long or the short date in Advanced, or click the date. The short date's order follows your browser unless you choose day-month-year, month-day-year or year-month-day. Clicking the date and the choices in Advanced stay in step. Show or hide the date under Date in Advanced.
- **Minimal**: shows the search, the clock and the colour button. Standard adds the date.
- **Colour button**: the switch under Show in Advanced is named Background colour button, since it shows or hides the colour button at the bottom left. The wallpaper button stays at the right when the colour button is hidden.
- **Icons**: Kagi, Lumo and Meta AI have monochrome icons in the same style as the built-in four, and the Kagi icon is sized to match.
- **Help, README and FAQ**: updated for the AI checklist, the new-tab options, the time and date choices, the export and import section, and the colour button.

## Privacy

Weather sends your IP address to ipapi.co, or the city you choose to Open-Meteo, only while weather is on. Showing your IP address uses the same lookup. Extra places send their name to Open-Meteo. A custom AI shortcut loads its icon from its own website (/favicon.ico) when it is added, so that site sees your visit. Nothing else leaves the page. Search and AI links go to the service you choose when you click.
