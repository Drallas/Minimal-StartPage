# v1.3

Since v1.2: the clock and the date can be set in Advanced, the date follows the browser, Minimal is leaner, and the icons and the phone layout are tidier.

## Highlights

- **Clock**: 24 hours, 12 hours (AM/PM) or full time with seconds, chosen in Advanced or by clicking the time. The colons stay still.
- **Date**: long or short, chosen in Advanced or by clicking the date. The order of the short date follows the browser unless you choose day-month-year, month-day-year or year-month-day. Clicking and Advanced stay in step. The date can be shown or hidden under Date in Advanced.
- **Minimal**: shows the search, the clock and the colour button. Standard adds the date.
- **Colour button**: the switch under Show in Advanced is named Background colour button. The wallpaper button stays at the right when the colour button is hidden.
- **Icons**: Kagi, Lumo and Meta AI have monochrome icons in the same style as the built-in four, and the Kagi icon is sized to match.
